# CSCI Undergrad (BSc) Portfolio

*by Patrick McGrath, Vancouver Island University ('21-'25), April 2026*

## Overview

This repository contains a selection of work I did pursuing a Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Computer Science at Vancouver Island University (VIU). The content consists of programs, code snippets and research papers. Some of my submissions to *Leetcode* problems are also included. 

All the work is my own, with the exception of some AI summaries. This is because this repository was made after the end of my course work, and I did not write summaries for all of my work at the time. Those summaries are noted. 

A separate repository of my geography work is available on this account. 

## About Me

My name is Patrick McGrath. I recently finished all my course work for my BSc in CS at VIU, and will be graduating in June of this year. As part of required electives, I studied mapping, geographic information systems (GIS) and remote sensing in the geography department. I found everything about it very fascinating. Therefore, I will be pursuing an Advanced Diploma in GIS at the British Columbia Institute of Technology (BCIT), September of this year.
