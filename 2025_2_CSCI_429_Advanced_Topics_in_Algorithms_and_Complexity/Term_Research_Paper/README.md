CSCI 429 - Advanced Topics in Algorithms and Complexity*, Fall, '25*

## *Overview*

*"This paper examines two scheduling algorithms—Coffman & Graham's Algorithm A (O(n²)) and Sethi's improved linear labeling and scheduling algorithm (O(nα(n)+e))—that optimally schedule computational tasks on two-processor systems represented as directed acyclic graphs. Both algorithms label tasks based on their partial order dependencies and then produce optimal schedules, with Sethi's approach achieving significantly better running time through clever use of levels, edge stacks, and disjoint set merging techniques."&#32;* [1]
